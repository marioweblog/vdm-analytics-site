VDM Analytics

A private, internal reporting tool.

VDM Analytics is a desktop reporting tool used internally by its owner to analyse the performance of their own websites and advertising campaigns. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does with Google

With the account owner's permission, VDM Analytics reads AdSense performance reports (earnings, impressions, page views and similar metrics, broken down by date, site, ad unit, country, platform, operating system and browser) through the AdSense Management API, using read-only access (adsense.readonly). It never changes anything in AdSense.

The data stays on the owner's own computer and is used only for the owner's own reporting.
